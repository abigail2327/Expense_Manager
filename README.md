**SpendLens**
A small web app that turns a bank or card statement into a clear picture of where your money goes. Upload a CSV, and SpendLens categorizes each transaction, charts your spending, and learns from your corrections.

**Features**
Upload a statement CSV, or click "Try sample data" to see a demo
Automatic categorization using merchant keywords (Lulu, Noon, Uber, Netflix and more)
Fix a category once and the merchant is remembered for all matching transactions
Summary cards: total spent, monthly average, biggest category, transaction count
Charts: spend by month, share by category, top merchants
Filter by date range and category
Add your own categories and merchant keywords
Download the categorized expenses as CSV, and your rules as JSON
Privacy: files are processed in memory and never stored

**Tech stack**
Python
Streamlit
pandas
Plotly


**Note on data**
The sample data is fictional. Do not commit real bank statements to this repository
